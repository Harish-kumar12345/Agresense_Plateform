# Data Source: All-India Soil Health Card (SHC) District Benchmark Dataset

## Source & Institutional Attribution
- **Dataset**: `backend/src/data/soilDistrictAverages.json` (794 agricultural districts across 36 Indian States & Union Territories)
- **Authority**: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
- **Portal**: https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in / National Soil Health Dashboard

## Coverage & Agro-Ecological Profiles
Covers 794 districts nationwide across Cycle-I (2015–2017) and Cycle-II (2017–2019) soil testing laboratory cycles:
1. **Indo-Gangetic Alluvial Plain**: (Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal)
   - Soil: Alluvial Loam to Sandy Loam
   - Benchmarks: pH 7.2–8.1, Available N: 160–220 kg/ha, Available P: 20–32 kg/ha, Available K: 180–260 kg/ha, Organic Carbon: 0.45–0.65%
2. **Deccan Plateau & Central Vertisols**: (Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, northern Karnataka, Telangana)
   - Soil: Deep Black Soil (Vertisols)
   - Benchmarks: pH 7.5–8.4, Available N: 140–190 kg/ha, Available P: 12–22 kg/ha, Available K: 240–380 kg/ha, Organic Carbon: 0.50–0.75%
3. **Red & Lateritic Zones**: (Tamil Nadu, southern Karnataka, Kerala, Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh)
   - Soil: Red Sandy Clay Loam / Acidic Laterite
   - Benchmarks: pH 5.2–6.8, Available N: 170–240 kg/ha, Available P: 14–26 kg/ha, Available K: 140–210 kg/ha, Organic Carbon: 0.55–0.95%
4. **Himalayan & Mountain Agro-Ecosystems**: (Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Jammu & Kashmir, North-Eastern States)
   - Soil: Brown Forest Soil / Mountain Loam
   - Benchmarks: pH 5.2–6.6, Available N: 215–310 kg/ha, Available P: 18–28 kg/ha, Available K: 160–240 kg/ha, Organic Carbon: 0.85–1.60%
5. **Arid & Western Desert**: (Rajasthan, southern Haryana)
   - Soil: Aridisols / Desert Sand
   - Benchmarks: pH 7.9–8.7, Available N: 110–155 kg/ha, Available P: 10–18 kg/ha, Available K: 190–280 kg/ha, Organic Carbon: 0.20–0.40%
