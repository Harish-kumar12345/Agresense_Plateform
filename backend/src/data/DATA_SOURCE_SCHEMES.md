# Data Source: National myScheme & DBT Agriculture Schemes Directory

## Source & Institutional Attribution
- **National Portal**: myScheme National Platform (https://myscheme.gov.in)
- **Central Ministry**: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India (https://agriwelfare.gov.in)
- **Direct Benefit Transfer**: DBT Bharat Portal (https://dbtbharat.gov.in)

## Content & Coverage
Contains 20+ verified central and state-specific agricultural schemes:
- **Central Flagship Programs**: PM-KISAN, PMFBY, Kisan Credit Card (KCC), SMAM (Mechanization), PM-KUSUM (Solar Pumps), PKVY (Organic Farming), PMKSY (Per Drop More Crop), Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF), Soil Health Card Scheme.
- **State-Specific Welfare Programs**:
  - Uttar Pradesh: UP Paradarshi Kisan Seva Yojana
  - Madhya Pradesh: Mukhyamantri Kisan Kalyan Yojana & Bhavantar Bhugtan Yojana
  - Haryana: Bhavantar Bharpai Yojana & Mera Pani Meri Virasat
  - Maharashtra: Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi
  - Rajasthan: Mukhyamantri Kisan Mitra Urja Yojana
  - Andhra Pradesh: YSR Rythu Bharosa
  - Telangana: Rythu Bandhu & Rythu Bima
  - West Bengal: Krishak Bandhu
  - Karnataka: Raitha Siri & Krishi Bhagya

## Schema Fields
- `id`: Unique scheme slug
- `name` / `title`: Official scheme designation
- `category`: Thematic classification (Income Support, Insurance, Machinery, Solar, Organic, etc.)
- `level`: 'Central' vs. 'State'
- `state`: 'All India' or specific Indian State
- `description`: Scope and objectives
- `eligibility`: Farmer beneficiary criteria
- `benefit`: Financial or input incentive details
- `link`: Official government application URL
- `active`: Operational status
