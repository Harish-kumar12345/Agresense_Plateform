# DATA SOURCE: Kisan Call Center (KCC) Agronomy Knowledge Base & ICAR POP

## 1. Source Origin
* **Portal / Agency**: Kisan Call Center (KCC) Knowledge Portal
* **Governing Body**: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (DA&FW), Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
* **Portal**: `https://dackkms.gov.in` and Open Government Data Platform (`https://data.gov.in`)
* **Supplementary Text**: ICAR Package of Practices (POP) & National Centre for Integrated Pest Management (NCIPM).

## 2. Ingestion & Scope
* **Topics Covered**:
  - Pest & Disease Diagnostic Identification and Treatment (Rice Blast, Sheath Blight, Wheat Yellow Rust, Pink Bollworm, Fall Armyworm, Sugarcane Red Rot).
  - Nutrient deficiencies (Zinc Khaira disease, Nitrogen chlorosis, Boron deficiency).
  - Soil Reclamation (Alkaline soil reclamation with Gypsum, Acidic soil reclamation with agricultural lime).
  - Central & State Agricultural Schemes (PM-KISAN DBT, PMFBY claim intimation, KCC loan subvention).
* **Format**: Indexed structured JSON articles with searchable agronomic tags for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

## 4. Refresh Cadence
* Curated and refreshed with new seasonal advisories and pest surveillance alerts.
