# Data Source: FAO-56 & ICAR Crop Agro-Climatic Parameters

## Source & Institutional Attribution
- **International Authority**: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) — Irrigation and Drainage Paper No. 56 (*Crop Evapotranspiration: Guidelines for Computing Crop Water Requirements*)
- **National Authority**: Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) — Agro-meteorology and Cropping Systems Research Division
- **Portals**: https://www.fao.org/land-water/databases-and-software/crop-information / https://icar.org.in
